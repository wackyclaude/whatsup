Whatsup — Family Dashboard

Whatsup is a private dashboard for one family. It runs in a web browser and can be installed as an app. After signing in with Google, it shows on one screen:

  • Today's calendar — events from the Google calendars you choose, read-only
  • Panchang — tithi, paksha, nakshatra, yoga, karana, Rahu Kaal and festivals
  • Sun & moon — sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset and tonight's moon phase
  • Weather — hour by hour, plus the next two days
  • India live camera — a live stream chosen by the family

Only Google accounts that the family has added to the dashboard's list can sign in. The app asks Google for permission to see your calendars and your email address. It can't change, delete or add anything.
