Privacy policy

Whatsup — Family Dashboard · Last updated 22 October 2026

Who this is for

Whatsup is a private dashboard run by one family for its own members. Only Google accounts the family has put on the dashboard's list can sign in.

What we access from your Google account

  • Your email address — only to check that your account is on the family's list.
  • Your calendars, read-only (calendar.readonly). The app reads today's and tomorrow's events from the calendars shown in your Google Calendar, so it can display them. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

The app does not access your Gmail, Drive, Photos, contacts or anything else.

How it's used and stored

  • Calendar events are shown on the dashboard screen. They are kept only briefly in the server's memory (about 15 minutes) to avoid asking Google too often, and are never written to a database.
  • To keep you signed in, your browser stores an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie holding your email address and a Google sign-in token. It can't be read by web pages and expires after 6 months.
  • Events that are marked Private in Google are shown only as "Busy".
  • The family can configure which calendars are hidden or shown as "Busy".

Sharing

Your Google data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. It's only shown on the dashboard to the people signed in to it.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the dashboard uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the app.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather forecast for the family's town. No personal data is sent.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) plays the live camera, if one is set up.

Your choices

  • Sign out on the dashboard removes the cookie from that device.
  • You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To be removed from the list, ask the family member who runs the dashboard.
